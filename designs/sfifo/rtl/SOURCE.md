`sfifo.v` is taken from ZipCPU wb2axip (https://github.com/ZipCPU/wb2axip, `rtl/sfifo.v`, commit 2e8d3bc2d26ddc33d1881022a2a2b9d3f0c16b9b). The design is placed in the public domain by its author, Dan Gisselquist (Gisselquist Technology, LLC). The only change is the removal of the `ifdef FORMAL` sections (the optional formal ports and the author's embedded properties), so that this study's own properties are the only ones checked. The RTL is otherwise unchanged.
